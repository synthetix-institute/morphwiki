## Identity, Realization And Completion

`K = Omega`, `M = (Omega, Xi)` and `I_op = (M; C,R,P)` are different identities.
A stable codeword records symbolic persistence at the run's resolution.
Physical mechanism identity additionally requires the tested interface to
carry a consequence. A named material realization includes variables, units,
domains, constitutive parameters, boundary/initial data, observable and ordered
protocol. Those details cannot be inferred from a token alone.

Joint destination scores and their marginals rank proposals. Record explicit
null factors, extraction uncertainty and unavailable evidence separately.
Report complete-paper held-out support for a learned relation. Examples from
the same training paper are not independent validation.

## Two Outcomes Of A Transfer Attempt

Supply source/target types and maps before evaluating
`Delta = Omega_B alpha - beta Omega_A`. Specify the norm, tolerance and domain
of evaluation. A valid change of realization requires dimensional and unit
consistency, relevant admissibility and the intended observable relation.

A nonzero residual can become a constructive result when it has:

1. a source-grounded operator definition;
2. a second equation providing algebraic closure, balance or evolution;
3. a clause assignment and a consequence that can be computed or measured.

`operator_promoted` means closure supplies an operator role.
`mechanism_extension` additionally requires a changed operational clause.
An assignment only to the realization layer does not redefine the mechanism.
Neither label establishes novelty in the literature or experimental success.

Do not replace a failed commutation test by a favorable pooled score. Record
the failure and the separate closed-defect argument. For composed paths, test
the assembled relation as well as its individual steps.

## Predictions And Controls

Use source-grounded relations to choose candidate equations and tests. A void
score is one optional selection source, not a prerequisite for all discovery.
Empty-cell counts are not evidence for inaccessible physical laws.

For a scaling proposal, fit the flanks separately, define the independent
quantities and validation interval, and distinguish interpolation from
extrapolation. An intersection of fitted laws is a crossover hypothesis, not
a demonstrated transition. Composition by eliminating a common variable is
an algebraic relation; predictive value needs a frozen fit and held-out test.

For a dynamical comparison, name preparation, release, observable and time.
Identical starts and paired noise isolate release from different writing
histories, but do not match target energy, torque or local curvature. A
topology-specific explanation needs controls for those competing mechanisms.
Analyze coupling strata before pooling. Related target families and reused
graphs are not independent systems. Do not relabel an unresolved crossover
as established by changing the verdict after seeing the same data.

## Deliverable

Write a physical account: system, operation, proposed change, governing
equations, assumptions, predicted observable, decisive control and evidence.
Use internal status names only in internal records. Keep confidence intervals,
failed tests and provenance available without turning scientific prose into a
workflow report. No simulation or fabrication backend status authorizes a
cluster launch, hardware action or publication by itself.
